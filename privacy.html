Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

  • Math with Sandy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
  • No ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Your child's progress stays on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Math with Sandy is a math practice game for 3rd graders, made by Tekumito (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the Math with Sandy app on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information, such as names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts, or device identifiers. There is no sign-up and no account.

The app does not connect to the internet to send or receive data. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

To keep the game going between sessions, the app saves a small amount of information on the device itself:

  • Game progress: stars earned, level, and results for each math topic
  • Settings: sound and vibration on or off
  • Sandy's outfit and spa visits

This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Reset all progress in the app's Grown-ups area, or by deleting the app.

Read-aloud

The app can read questions out loud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. The app does not send the text anywhere; speech is handled by your device's operating system under its own settings.

Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. Microphone access is specifically turned off.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no social features, and no in-app purchases. No information is shared with or sold to third parties.

Children's privacy

Math with Sandy is made for children. Because the app collects no personal information, it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the requirements of the Apple App Store Kids category and Google Play's Families program.

Settings and progress controls are kept in a Grown-ups area behind a parent gate (a math problem meant for adults), so children can't change them by accident. Links that leave the app are also behind the parent gate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts handling information differently, we will update this policy before that change reaches the app.
